Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How many inches are in one foot?
Exactly 12 inches are in one standard foot (1 ft = 12 in).
How many meters are in a kilometer?
There are 1,000 meters in one kilometer (1 km = 1,000 m).
What is the difference between a 225 and 272 sq ft Marla?
Private urban housing schemes (such as DHA and Bahria Town) typically standardize 1 Marla at 225 square feet, while government land revenue departments traditionally measure 1 Marla as 272.25 square feet. OmniCalc allows you to choose either measurement.
